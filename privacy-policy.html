Privacy Policy

Trac Logistics ("Trac," "we," "us") operates the Trac Logistics mobile app, a platform connecting customers who need to send packages ("Customers") with independent transporters ("Transporters") who deliver them across Nigeria. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Account information

  • Full name, email address, and phone number
  • Your password, stored as a secure one-way hash — we never store or have access to your actual password
  • If you sign in with Google, your Google account identifier
  • Your profile photo, bio, and your rating/trip history on the platform

1.2 Transporter verification (Transporters only)

  • Your National Identification Number (NIN) and driver's license number, verified through our identity-verification partner, QoreID
  • A photo of your physical driver's license
  • Vehicle details (type, plate number, year) and, if applicable, your company name and RC (business registration) number

1.3 Location information

  • Pickup and delivery coordinates you provide when booking a delivery
  • For Transporters: your live GPS location, collected continuously — including while the app is in the background — only while you have an active delivery assignment. This lets the Customer track their delivery on a live map in real time. A persistent notification stays visible on your device whenever background location sharing is active, and sharing stops as soon as the delivery is complete.

1.4 Payment and payout information

  • Card and bank details you enter to pay for a delivery are collected and processed directly by our payment processor, Paystack — Trac's own servers never receive or store your card number
  • We store the outcome of each transaction (amount, status, reference code), your in-app wallet balance, and any promotional or referral credit
  • For Transporters: your bank account name and number, used (via Paystack) to pay out your delivery earnings

1.5 Communications

  • Messages sent through in-app chat with your delivery counterpart are stored so both parties and our support team can refer back to them, including to help resolve disputes. For your safety, we automatically filter attempts to share phone numbers or third-party messaging contacts in chat.
  • Voice calls between Customers and Transporters are carried in real time through our calling partner, Agora. We do not record or store call audio.

1.6 Notifications

We store your device's push-notification token and a history of notifications sent to you, so we can keep you updated on bids, deliveries, and payments.

1.7 Referrals

If you refer someone to Trac, we track the two accounts involved and the status of any referral reward.

1.8 Technical and crash data

We use Sentry to automatically collect crash reports and performance data if the app encounters an error, to help us find and fix bugs. This may include device information and the sequence of app actions leading up to the crash.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the core service: matching Customers with Transporters, processing deliveries, and handling payments
  • To verify Transporter identity and vehicle details, for the safety of everyone using Trac
  • To show live delivery tracking to Customers
  • To process payments and send Transporter payouts
  • To send you notifications about your deliveries, bids, and account
  • To investigate disputes, fraud, or violations of our Terms of Service
  • To maintain and improve the app, including diagnosing crashes and bugs
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Who we share information with

We share information with the following service providers, only as needed to operate Trac:

  • QoreID — identity verification (NIN / driver's license)
  • Paystack — payment processing and Transporter payouts
  • Agora — real-time voice call connection (call audio is relayed only, never stored)
  • Google — sign-in and map services
  • Sentry — crash and error reporting
  • Supabase — secure database hosting
  • Expo / EAS — push notification delivery and app updates

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may also share information where required by law, to protect the rights and safety of Trac, our users, or the public, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

4. Data retention

We keep your account and transaction information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward for legal, safety, fraud-prevention, and dispute-resolution purposes — for example, records of completed deliveries, payments, and related chat history.

When you delete your account in the app, we immediately deactivate it and sign it out of every device — it can no longer be used to log in. If you would like us to erase specific personal data beyond what we are required to retain, contact us at [email protected] and we will address your request in line with applicable law.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, or to object to certain uses of it. You can update most account details directly in the app, or contact us at [email protected] for anything else.

6. Children's privacy

Trac is not directed at, and may not be used by, anyone under 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. International data transfers

Some of our service providers — including our database host, payment processor, and other infrastructure partners — may process data outside Nigeria. Where this happens, we take steps to ensure your information continues to be protected in line with this policy.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) between the app and our servers and secure password hashing. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data appropriately.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you in the app or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or how we handle your information? Contact us at [email protected].
