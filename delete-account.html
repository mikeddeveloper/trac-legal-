Delete Your Account

Last updated: October 8, 2026
← Trac Logistics legal

You can request deletion of your Trac Logistics account and its associated data at any time, either from within the app or by contacting us directly.

How to delete your account

Option 1 — In the app

  • Open the Trac Logistics app and sign in
  • Go to Settings
  • Tap Delete account
  • Confirm when prompted

Option 2 — Can't access the app?

Email us at [email protected] from the address associated with your account and request account deletion. We'll process your request promptly.

What happens when you delete your account

  • Your account is deactivated immediately and you're signed out of every device — it can no longer be used to log in.
  • Your profile (name, email, phone number, profile photo, bio) is no longer accessible or shown to other users of the app.

What data is deleted, what's retained, and for how long

Deleted / no longer accessible immediately: login access to your account, and your visibility to other users (your profile is no longer shown to anyone).

Retained for a period after deletion: records connected to deliveries you've completed — including delivery and payment history, in-app chat tied to those deliveries, and (for Transporters) identity-verification status and vehicle details — are kept for legal, safety, fraud-prevention, accounting, and dispute-resolution purposes, consistent with our Privacy Policy. This data is not shown to other users or usable to log back in; it is retained internally only as required.

Requesting full erasure

If you'd like us to erase specific personal data beyond what we're required to retain, contact us at [email protected] and we'll address your request in line with applicable law.
